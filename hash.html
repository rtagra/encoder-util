One input, both hashes at once — MD5 and SHA-256, in your browser.

MD5 is fast but broken for security — use it for checksums / fingerprints only, not passwords. For a secure hash, use SHA-256. SHA-256 uses Web Crypto, so open over https:// or http://localhost — not file://.

Browser-only: everything runs in your browser. No server, nothing is sent anywhere.
